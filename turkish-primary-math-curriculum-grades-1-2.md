# Turkish Primary Mathematics Curriculum Outline: Grades 1 and 2

This document summarizes the current Turkish primary mathematics curriculum outlines for Grade 1 and Grade 2 in English. It is based on the Ministry of National Education's Türkiye Yüzyılı Maarif Model pages and supporting curriculum pages that reproduce the MEB learning outcomes.

## Source Notes

- Official course program page: Türkiye Yüzyılı Maarif Model, Primary School Mathematics.
- Official grade pages list the themes for Grade 1 and Grade 2.
- Detailed learning outcomes below are translated from Turkish curriculum text published as MEB-based learning outcomes.
- Turkish terms:
  - `Tema` = Theme
  - `Ders Saati` = Lesson hours
  - `Öğrenme Çıktısı` = Learning outcome

## Grade 1 Mathematics

Grade 1 mathematics consists of 7 themes, 19 learning outcomes, and 180 lesson hours.

### Theme 1: Numbers and Quantities (1)

Lesson hours: 57

Focus: Students use digits and numbers to represent quantities; count object groups up to 20; understand order; compare quantities; count rhythmically up to 100 forward and from 20 backward; analyze number and shape patterns; and estimate quantities up to 20.

Learning outcomes:

- `MAT.1.1.1`: Use digits and numbers up to and including 20 to represent the sizes of quantities.
- `MAT.1.1.2`: Analyze relationships between parts while counting a group of objects arranged regularly or irregularly.
- `MAT.1.1.3`: Show the ordinal position of objects.
- `MAT.1.1.4`: Compare two quantities using terms such as more, fewer, less, and equal.
- `MAT.1.1.5`: Count rhythmically forward up to 100 and backward from 20.
- `MAT.1.1.6`: Analyze increasing or decreasing number and shape patterns.
- `MAT.1.1.7`: Estimate the number of objects up to and including 20 by using relationships within a given set.

### Theme 2: Numbers and Quantities (2)

Lesson hours: 18

Focus: Students estimate the results of measuring length and mass using appropriate non-standard measuring tools.

Learning outcomes:

- `MAT.1.1.8`: Estimate length and mass measurement results using appropriate non-standard measuring tools.

### Theme 3: Numbers and Quantities (3)

Lesson hours: 7

Focus: Students recognize the quantities represented by Turkish lira banknotes and coins used in daily life.

Learning outcomes:

- `MAT.1.1.9`: Recognize the values represented by Turkish money: 1 TL, 5 TL, 10 TL, 20 TL, 50 TL, 100 TL, and 200 TL.

### Theme 4: From Operations to Algebraic Thinking

Lesson hours: 57

Focus: Students analyze addition and subtraction in daily-life contexts; perform addition and subtraction within 20; interpret equality; reason through estimates and mental computation; understand the relationship between addition and subtraction; recognize the commutative property of addition; identify missing terms; and solve simple problem situations with addition and subtraction.

Learning outcomes:

- `MAT.1.2.1`: Analyze addition and subtraction operations embedded in daily-life situations.
- `MAT.1.2.2`: Reason about the results of addition and subtraction by estimating and using mental computation.
- `MAT.1.2.3`: Interpret the meaning of the equal sign in the context of addition and subtraction.
- `MAT.1.2.4`: Interpret the relationship between addition and subtraction.

### Theme 5: Geometry of Objects (1)

Lesson hours: 15

Focus: Students use concepts of place, direction, and position; connect instructions with a starting point and target; follow directions to reach a target; and evaluate whether objects match using visual criteria such as color, size, and shape.

Learning outcomes:

- `MAT.1.3.1`: Analyze instructions involving distances and directions in order to reach a target.
- `MAT.1.3.2`: Evaluate the congruence or matching of objects using selected criteria.

### Theme 6: Geometry of Objects (2)

Lesson hours: 15

Focus: Students distinguish everyday objects by form; identify geometric shapes in geometric structures using corners and sides; and classify triangles, squares, rectangles, and circles according to their formal properties.

Learning outcomes:

- `MAT.1.3.3`: Distinguish everyday objects according to formal properties.
- `MAT.1.3.4`: Analyze geometric shapes in everyday geometric structures.
- `MAT.1.3.5`: Classify geometric shapes according to formal properties.

### Theme 7: Data-Based Research

Lesson hours: 10

Focus: Students conduct the steps of a statistical investigation with one categorical data group.

Learning outcomes:

- `MAT.1.4.1`: Work with a basic categorical data group and make data-based decisions.

## Grade 2 Mathematics

Grade 2 mathematics consists of 6 themes, 25 learning outcomes, and 180 lesson hours.

### Theme 1: Numbers and Quantities (1)

Lesson hours: 41

Focus: Students use numbers up to 100 to represent quantities; decompose object groups up to 100; determine number order; count rhythmically forward and backward; make inferences from number and shape patterns; and estimate quantities up to 50.

Learning outcomes:

- `MAT.2.1.1`: Use symbolic representations of numbers to represent quantities up to 100.
- `MAT.2.1.2`: Decompose two-digit numbers into tens and ones and identify place values.
- `MAT.2.1.3`: Determine the order of numbers.
- `MAT.2.1.4`: Count rhythmically forward and backward.
- `MAT.2.1.5`: Make inferences based on number patterns and shape patterns that transform into number representations.
- `MAT.2.1.6`: Estimate the number of objects up to 50 by using relationships within a set.

### Theme 2: Numbers and Quantities (2)

Lesson hours: 30

Focus: Students understand whole, half, and quarter relationships; relate and decompose money values; read and write time units; recognize the need for standard tools and units for length and mass; and estimate length and mass using standard units.

Learning outcomes:

- `MAT.2.1.7`: Analyze the relationship between whole, half, and quarter.
- `MAT.2.1.8`: Relate and decompose money according to value, including Turkish lira and kurus relationships.
- `MAT.2.1.9`: Read and write units of time.
- `MAT.2.1.10`: Explain the need for standard length and mass measuring tools and units.
- `MAT.2.1.11`: Estimate lengths and masses using standard length and mass units.

### Theme 3: From Operations to Algebraic Thinking

Lesson hours: 55

Focus: Students solve daily-life problems involving addition and subtraction; estimate and mentally reason about addition and subtraction; interpret the relationship between addition and subtraction; understand multiplication as repeated addition and division as repeated subtraction; estimate and reason about multiplication and division; and interpret equality in the context of the four operations.

Learning outcomes:

- `MAT.2.2.1`: Solve daily-life problems requiring addition and subtraction.
- `MAT.2.2.2`: Reason about the results of addition and subtraction by estimating and using mental computation.
- `MAT.2.2.3`: Interpret the relationship between addition and subtraction.
- `MAT.2.2.4`: Analyze multiplication and division based on addition and subtraction.
- `MAT.2.2.5`: Reason about the results of multiplication and division.
- `MAT.2.2.6`: Interpret different meanings of equality in the context of the four operations.

### Theme 4: Geometry of Objects (1)

Lesson hours: 25

Focus: Students classify everyday objects as geometric solids; build structures with geometric solid models; build models with geometric shapes; understand that formal properties remain unchanged when direction, position, or size changes; and estimate liquid quantities using non-standard measuring tools.

Learning outcomes:

- `MAT.2.3.1`: Classify everyday objects as geometric solids according to formal properties.
- `MAT.2.3.2`: Synthesize structures using geometric solid models.
- `MAT.2.3.3`: Synthesize models using geometric shapes.
- `MAT.2.3.4`: Interpret that the formal properties of geometric solids and shapes do not change when their direction, position, or size changes.
- `MAT.2.3.5`: Estimate liquid quantity using non-standard liquid measuring tools.

### Theme 5: Geometry of Objects (2)

Lesson hours: 11

Focus: Students choose strategies to reach a target using distance and direction, and distinguish symmetrical shapes.

Learning outcomes:

- `MAT.2.3.6`: Decide on appropriate strategies to reach a target using distance and direction.
- `MAT.2.3.7`: Distinguish symmetrical shapes from a given set of shapes.

### Theme 6: Data-Based Research

Lesson hours: 10

Focus: Students conduct the steps of a statistical investigation with up to two categorical data groups.

Learning outcomes:

- `MAT.2.4.1`: Work with up to two categorical data groups and make data-based decisions.
